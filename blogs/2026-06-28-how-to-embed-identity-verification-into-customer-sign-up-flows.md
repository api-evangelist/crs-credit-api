Integrating identity verification directly into account creation flows, rather than as a separate step afterward, helps prevent fraud while reducing friction for legitimate users. The strongest approach combines identity checks, fraud screening, and credit data in a single API call, with fraud detection running before credit pulls to filter risk early and protect good customers from exposure to bad actors.
