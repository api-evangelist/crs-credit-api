The article distinguishes between two types of credit inquiries used in mortgage lending. A soft pull provides a credit preview without affecting the borrower's score and suits prequalification, while a tri-merge pull combines reports from all three credit bureaus into one underwriting-grade document required for formal application and lending decisions. Lenders use both strategically: soft pulls early to protect scores during shopping, tri-merge pulls later for the actual underwriting decision.
